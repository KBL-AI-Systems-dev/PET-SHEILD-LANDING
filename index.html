2 AM Red-Flag Quick Check

  • 1. Gum Color: Healthy gums are bubblegum pink. Pale, white, blue, or brick-red gums = immediate ER visit.
  • 2. Resting Breathing: Over 35–40 breaths/min at rest or flared nostrils/belly effort = immediate ER visit.
  • 3. Unproductive Retching: Trying to vomit with nothing coming up (especially deep-chested dogs) = immediate ER visit.
  • 4. Urinary Straining: Repeated litter-box trips with no urine (especially male cats) = immediate ER visit.
